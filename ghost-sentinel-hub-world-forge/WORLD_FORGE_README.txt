GHOST SENTINEL HUB — NEW INTERACTIVE WORLD FORGE
=================================================

START
  Type: !build world

HOW TO ANSWER
  First choose Beginner (25), Medium (50), or Advanced (100).
  Every question displays exactly 20 choices.
  Answer with one number, several numbers such as 1,4,7, the displayed words,
  or your own written answer.

CONTROLS DURING THE QUESTIONNAIRE
  back      Return to the previous question
  show      Display all answers recorded so far
  restart   Erase the current answers and start again
  cancel    Stop without creating a world

FINISH
  After the final preview, type yes to create and save the world.
  Type back to revise the last response.

HELP
  Type: !help

The completed forge saves the world as the active world for the current room.
Its questionnaire level, question count, population, terrain, society,
settlements, architecture, creatures, danger, time, and growth settings are
connected to the World Directory, World Statistics, map, PBX, and export.

VISUAL ROBLOX / SIMCITY ENGINE
  1. Start Ghost Sentinel Hub normally.
  2. Open the Hub in your browser.
  3. Press "Open Roblox / SimCity World Engine" at the top.

  Build roads and zone residential, commercial, or industrial land. Zoned
  tiles touching roads grow automatically. Use WASD or the arrow keys to move
  the player, E to open a building door, and the numbered keys to select tools.
  At normal speed one simulated year takes about eight minutes.

  VIEW CONTROLS
    Map          Isometric building and zoning view
    Street View  Ground-level view from the player's current tile
    Star Chart   World constellation and neighboring travel routes
    Zoom -/+     Zoom from 55% to 225%; the mouse wheel also zooms

  Townspeople and wildlife walk around automatically. Street View follows the
  same player position as the map. Use Q and R to turn while in Street View.

PLAYER SWEEP
  The World Engine sends a heartbeat while a player is active. Press
  "Sweep inactive players" to archive records with no heartbeat for 30 minutes.
  Archived records are retained for recovery and are purged after 30 days.
  A returning player becomes active again automatically.

LIVING WORLD + HOME DESIGNER
  Use "Add 16×16 district" repeatedly whenever the city needs more land.
  Pan between districts with North/West/East/South and use Center to return to
  your player. Select "Build custom home," choose its name/style/floors, then
  click a map tile. The new home opens directly in Interior view.

  Interior rooms and furniture autosave with the world. Choose a room type and
  place beds, headboards, end tables, dressers, bathroom fixtures, sofas,
  tables, chairs, lamps, rugs, and plants. Select a home on the map or stand on
  it and press E to reopen its interior later.

TEN DEVELOPED SHOWCASE WORLDS
  The first server launch automatically creates Aurora Harbor, Emerald Vale,
  Sunfire Metropolis, Moonstone Citadel, Sapphire Coast, Rosewood Gardens,
  Crystal Heights, Golden Prairie, Starlight Basin, and Frostpine Capital.
  Choose one from the Showcase Worlds selector and press Visit.

  Every showcase includes a 64×48 developed city, river or coast, connected
  roads, parks, residential neighborhoods, downtown towers, industry, three
  landmarks, four furnished showcase homes, 70 moving citizens/animals, and a
  distinct artistic color and lighting theme. Existing worlds are not replaced.

OPEN-WORLD STREET NAVIGATION
  In Street View, Up/W walks forward and Down/S walks backward relative to the
  direction you face. Left/Right turns, A/D steps sideways, Shift runs two
  tiles, and E enters an available home. The on-screen direction pad provides
  the same controls for touch or mouse use.

  Street View includes sidewalks, lanes, traffic, pedestrians, streetlights,
  building signs, illuminated windows, themed weather colors, day/night skies,
  and a rotating minimap. Buildings and water block movement, so streets and
  walkable open land form the navigation routes.

VISUAL WORLD BUILDER WIZARD
  Press "Create a new world" in the World Directory. The four-step wizard asks
  for identity/theme, terrain/size, development/population/home style/speed,
  and then displays a final preview. Press Create World to forge and open it.

  Terrain choices are River City, Ocean Coast, Lake Country, Island Realm, and
  Mountain Valleys. Starting sizes are 64×48, 80×64, and 96×80, and every size
  can continue expanding in 16×16 districts. Creating a duplicate name makes a
  separate numbered world instead of overwriting the original.

INTERIOR DECORATOR FIX
  Press Interior to automatically open the nearest home, or choose any building
  from the Home to Decorate list. Older residential buildings are upgraded with
  their own interior the first time they are selected. Choose furniture, then
  click inside the floor plan to place it. Each home's furniture saves separately.
